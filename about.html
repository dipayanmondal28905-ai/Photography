OUR STORY

We don't just take pictures.

Every wedding is different. Every family has its own chaos. Every couple has their own language of love.

At Chirkut, we believe our job is not simply to stand behind a camera and document what happens. Our job is to notice what others might miss.

A mother's expression before the bride walks in. A nervous smile from the groom. Friends laughing in the corner. A hand being held during a quiet moment.

These are the moments that eventually become the most valuable memories.

Founder & Creative Director

Ritam's approach to photography starts long before the shutter is pressed.
